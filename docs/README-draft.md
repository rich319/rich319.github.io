## What I've built

### Onboarding enrichment stack
A Relevance AI pipeline that researches new signups, writes enrichment data back into HubSpot, and generates a fully personalised onboarding email for each customer. Not segmented — individually written, from their data.

Over 60 days, against a control group receiving standard onboarding emails:

| | AI emails | Control |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 27.1% | 17.0% |
| Click rate | 2.31% | 0.41% |

+10.1pp on opens, +1.90pp on clicks. Both statistically significant (p < 0.05).

---

### Call intelligence pipeline
Ingests sales and customer success call recordings. Surfaces feature requests, cross-references them against our knowledge base, adds gaps to the Linear backlog, clusters semantically similar tickets, then triggers automated competitive and codebase investigations to help prioritise.

Customer voice → prioritised backlog. Minimal human handling required.

---

### Launch orchestration agent
An AI agent that turns a Linear ticket into a fully written go-to-market project. Feed it a ticket. It reads the spec, sizes the launch, then creates a Notion project with every touchpoint drafted — email copy, social posts, in-app guidance, Amplitude tracking specs, help centre articles, battle cards.

Not stubs. Not templates. The actual content, ready for review.

Connects Linear, Notion, Amplitude, and HubSpot. Repo at [DialogueConsulting/claude-code-config](https://github.com/DialogueConsulting/claude-code-config).

---

### Feature adoption agent
Handles everything after launch: Amplitude funnel analysis to find where users drop off, cohort targeting, in-app intervention copy, behavioural HubSpot email sequences, A/B experiment setup, and a fortnightly review that reads results and tells you whether to iterate or close.

Launch gets the feature out. Adoption makes it stick.

---

### Backlog consolidation agent
Scans the Linear feature backlog for semantically similar issues. Auto-merges exact duplicates without human input. Surfaces near-matches — grouped by product pillar and strategic context — for review. Keeps the backlog clean without losing signal.

---

### Feature investigation agent
Given a Linear ticket that's flagged for investigation, produces a one-page prioritisation report: competitive landscape across 3–7 competitors, technical feasibility via codebase search, usage signal from Amplitude, and customer signal from HubSpot. Three modes — full, competitive, or feasibility — depending on what the decision actually needs. Findings post directly back to the ticket.
